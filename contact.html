LET'S MAKE PLAY BETTER

We'd like to hear from you.

Found a problem or have an idea for the next game? Email our support team at customerarcadesupport@gmail.com.

Email support

For game help, feedback, privacy questions, or sponsorship inquiries, contact customerarcadesupport@gmail.com.

Email ArcadeGamingWorld ↗

Your email app will open so you can review and send your message.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. If you attach a screenshot, remove personal information first.

Report a game problem

These details help us understand what went wrong:

  • The name of the game and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps that caused the problem and any error message you saw.
  • Your browser and whether you were using a phone, tablet, or computer.
  • An optional screenshot with personal information removed.
Report a problem by email ↗

Ideas & feedback

Suggest a game, a new control option, or a way to make playing more accessible. Email us and describe your idea.

Other questions

For general, privacy, or sponsorship questions, email customerarcadesupport@gmail.com. Include a clear subject so we can understand your request.

Can't see a new game?

First select All games, clear the search box, and reload the page. If your browser still shows an older version, try a private browsing window. There is no need to share your browsing history or account details when reporting the problem.
