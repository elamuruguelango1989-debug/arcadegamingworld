Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ArcadeGamingWorld (arcadegamingworld.com) handles information when you browse and play our browser games. You can play without registering for an account. Some optional features, such as public leaderboards and shared game favorites, use Cloudflare-hosted services.

1. Games and accounts

You do not need an ArcadeGamingWorld account to play. Our games do not require you to provide your legal name, email address, payment information, or precise location. Most gameplay happens in your browser. Certain optional online features transmit limited information as described below.

2. Browser storage and personal game progress

Some games store personal-best scores and progress in your browser's local storage, including keys beginning with agw-. Your browser may also store favorite selections, sound preferences, daily challenge progress, streaks, and achievements. These settings help remember your choices on the same browser and device.

You can clear these records using your browser's site-data settings for arcadegamingworld.com. Doing so may reset saved scores, preferences, streaks, and achievements. Private browsing or browser restrictions may prevent these features from persisting. Locally saved personal bests are separate from scores you voluntarily submit to a public leaderboard.

3. Shared favorites and community features

ArcadeGamingWorld offers shared game-favorite counts. When you interact with a shared favorite feature, your browser may communicate the relevant game selection to a Cloudflare-hosted endpoint so the site can update or display aggregate counts. Your own favorite selections may also be stored in your browser. These features do not require an account. We do not intend for favorite counts to reveal the identity of individual players.

4. Optional public leaderboard

If you choose to submit a Skyline Sprint (Endless Runner) score, the nickname you provide and your score may be shown publicly. Please use an alias and do not include your real name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in a nickname.

The Cloudflare-backed leaderboard uses limited request information to support score verification and abuse prevention. The currently documented implementation creates a salted daily identifier derived from a network address rather than storing raw IP addresses in its leaderboard database. This may cause people on the same network to share a leaderboard slot. The leaderboard's run records are intended to expire after one day and score records after seven days, with cleanup triggered when new runs begin. Cloudflare may separately process technical request logs under its own practices. For score removal requests, email customerarcadesupport@gmail.com.

5. Hosting and third-party services

The public website is delivered using Cloudflare, including Cloudflare Pages and services used for optional shared features. Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request metadata to deliver and secure the site. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Source code may be maintained on GitHub; that does not mean the live site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

The website may request fonts from Google Fonts. If so, your browser connects to Google's servers to retrieve font files, and Google may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Analytics choices

ArcadeGamingWorld may offer optional audience analytics. Where the site provides an Allow analytics choice, Google Analytics should load only when that feature has been configured and you have allowed it. When enabled, it may measure page visits, game opens, supported game starts and completed runs, and recommendation clicks. Your choice may be stored in your browser and may be changed through an Analytics preferences control where available.

Cloudflare may also provide hosting-related traffic and security measurements. The specific analytics services in operation can change; this policy should be updated to reflect the actual production configuration. If optional analytics is not configured, that integration does not send Google Analytics events.

7. Advertising and cookies

ArcadeGamingWorld may introduce third-party advertising in the future, including Google AdSense. This section does not mean that AdSense is currently active. Before advertising is enabled, we will update this policy as needed to identify relevant advertising partners and explain how cookies or similar technologies may be used to serve, measure, and personalize ads. We will also implement applicable notice, consent, and privacy-choice mechanisms where required.

For information about Google's advertising practices, see How Google uses information in advertising. You can manage certain Google ad preferences at My Ad Center. Browser settings may also let you restrict or delete cookies and site data.

8. Daily challenges and sharing

Daily challenge progress, streaks, and achievements may be saved in your browser. If you use a share or copy button, sharing occurs only when you take that action. The destination app or service handles anything you choose to send. Choosing Share on Facebook opens Facebook with a public game link; nothing is posted automatically by ArcadeGamingWorld.

9. Contact messages

For privacy questions or support, email customerarcadesupport@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you send. We use this information to respond and follow up as needed. Correspondence is handled using Gmail, subject to Google's Privacy Policy, and retained as needed for support and related records. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

10. Children's privacy

Our games do not require players to provide personal information to play. Children should not submit identifying information through leaderboard nicknames or support messages. Parents and guardians may contact us with concerns without providing unnecessary information about a child. We review our audience, advertising, and analytics practices for applicable children's privacy requirements; these requirements may affect which features or advertising technologies can be enabled.

11. Your choices

You can play without an account, remove individual favorites, clear browser storage, decline optional analytics where the choice is offered, and avoid public leaderboard submissions. To ask about leaderboard removal, support correspondence, or another privacy concern, contact customerarcadesupport@gmail.com.

12. External links

Links to third-party sites are subject to those sites' privacy policies. Opening an external link may share normal browser request information with the destination service.

13. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our games, analytics, advertising, or service providers change. The updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

Questions? Contact customerarcadesupport@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
