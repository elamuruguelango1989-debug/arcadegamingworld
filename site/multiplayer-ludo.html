Play live with 2–4 friends on different devices. No account required.

Players

    How to play

    Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the track. Move by the dice value, capture opponents on non-safe squares, and get all four tokens home with an exact roll to win. Rolling a 6 gives an extra turn (up to three rolls in a turn).

    This is a streamlined multiplayer Ludo variant. It has no betting or cash prizes. Room members can reconnect using the same browser.
