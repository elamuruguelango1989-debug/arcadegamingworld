Minesweeper

Reveal all 54 safe squares without revealing one of the ten mines.

How to play

Tap a square to reveal it. A number counts mines in the neighboring eight squares. Use Flag mode or right-click to mark a suspected mine. Turn Flag mode off to reveal again.

A useful tip

Your first reveal and its neighboring squares are safe. A square with no neighboring mines opens nearby safe squares automatically. Flags are reminders, not proof that a square contains a mine.

Another round: You can place up to ten flags. New game creates a new field. Revealing a mine ends the round.
