RED’S TURN

Roll into a good time.

✦

Red starts. Roll a six to leave your yard.

The home stretch

Get all four tokens home to win. Colors also have letter labels: R, G, Y and B.

How to play

  1. Roll a six to bring a token out. Then tap a glowing token or its move button.
  2. Travel clockwise. Your colored lane leads to home; you need the exact roll to finish.
  3. Land on a rival to send them back to their yard. Star squares are safe and can be shared.
  4. A six, a capture, or a token reaching home earns another roll. Three consecutive sixes skip the third move and end your turn.
  5. There are no blockades: stacked tokens do not stop movement. On an unsafe square, a capture sends all rival tokens there back.
  6. No legal move? The game passes automatically (a six still earns another roll). First to bring four tokens home wins.

A casual house-rules edition. Same-device play only; no online rooms yet. Match progress lasts for this page visit.
