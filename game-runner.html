HOW TO PLAY

  • Space, ↑ or W: jump; press again in the air for a double jump.
  • ↓ or S: slide for a short time while on the ground.
  • Phone: use Jump and Slide. Tapping the field also jumps.
  • Orange crates: jump over them.
  • Pink laser gates: stay on the ground and slide beneath them.
  • P: pause or resume. Escape exits full-window mode.

Speed increases as you run. Sound is optional and starts off.
